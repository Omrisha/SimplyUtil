# SimplyUtil — Support & Info

Welcome! This is the official support page for the SimplyUtil app.

SimplyUtil is your travel companion that helps you discover cities around the world—complete with weather forecasts, landmarks, currency exchange rates, and more.

## Key Features
- Browse cities worldwide with detailed information
- Check current weather forecasts for any city
- Discover landmarks and points of interest
- View real-time currency exchange rates
- Save your favorite cities for quick access
- Clean, intuitive design built with SwiftUI

## Getting Started
1. Browse the list of cities or search for a specific destination.
2. Tap on a city to view detailed information including weather and landmarks.
3. Mark cities as favorites by tapping the heart icon for easy access later.
4. Check exchange rates to help plan your travel budget.

## FAQ
- Where is my data stored?
  - Your favorite cities are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If iCloud is enabled, favorites may sync across your Apple devices.
- Do you track my location?
  - No. We do not track your GPS location or collect personal information.
- Is my data shared with third parties?
  - No. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with anyone.
- Where does the weather and city data come from?
  - We fetch data from our backend server, which aggregates information from public APIs.

## Privacy & Security

### What We Collect
- Favorite cities (stored locally on your device)
- Server logs for debugging (anonymized, deleted after 30 days)

### What We DON'T Collect
- Your precise location or GPS data
- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Usage analytics or tracking cookies
- Advertising identifiers
- Payment information (the app is free)

### Data Protection
- All communication uses HTTPS encryption
- Your favorites are protected by your device's built-in encryption
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties
- Open source—review our code on GitHub

### iCloud Sync
If iCloud is enabled on your device, your favorite cities may sync across your Apple devices. This is controlled by your iOS settings. See Apple's [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details.

For complete privacy details, contact support or review our [full privacy policy](#privacy--security).
